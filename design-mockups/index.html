Bringing the lounge onto the website

Four directions for the homepage, based on the photos of the lounge and Brightwall's mural. These are mockups only. They don't change the real site, and all content is the same as of now.

What the lounge looks like

Light and airy

Pale mint-grey walls, lots of daylight. The current site is mostly black and cream with torn-paper edges, which reads more "dark coffee house" than the lounge does.

Flat, painted shapes

Big soft lavender leaves and bold cutout flowers in mustard, periwinkle, sage and orange, with each cat sitting on its own colored shape. Very Matisse-like.

Playful hand lettering

"Will Feral" and "Deliver to: Kitty Brew Cat Café" are painted in a casual hand-drawn style. A handwritten accent font captures that.

Warm birch wood

The big plywood cat tree, pegboard shelves and rounded cutouts add a warm, natural counterpoint to the cool walls.

A · Mural Wall

The site feels painted on the lounge walls: mint background, scattered leaves and flowers, colorful headings, rounded buttons, and photos in organic shapes.

Biggest change · most "you are there"

B · Birch & Blooms

Warm and woody like the cat tree and gift shop: cream background, plywood-style panels, arched photo windows, a pegboard photo wall, and teal accents.

Cozy · photo-forward

C · Fresh Coat

Today's layout, repainted: indigo instead of black, mint instead of cream, mural orange buttons, and wavy lavender edges instead of torn paper.

Smallest change · easiest to apply

D · Painted Fresh Coat

C's familiar layout (split hero, indigo blocks, wavy edge) with A's personality: painted cats crossing the photos, a colorful headline, hand lettering, rounded buttons and the mural band.

Best of both · recognizable but new
