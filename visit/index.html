Policies

Cat Lounge Visits

Each visit includes 50 minutes in the cat lounge, where you can unwind with a drink and enjoy time with our adorable feline friends.

What to Expect

We typically have 20–25 cats in the lounge. That number may vary depending on the adoption rate.

Some cats will be social and playful, while others may prefer to nap or observe. Just like people, every cat has their own personality.

Admission

$13 per person

Admission is the same price every day, except Wednesdays, when kids age 15 and under can join for just $7 as part of our Kids Day.

Cancellations & Refunds

  • Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before your visit to receive a refund.
  • Refunds are minus a 5% credit card processing fee, which is no longer refunded by Square.
  • No-shows and same-day cancellations are non-refundable.
  • Rescheduling is always free!
  • To cancel or reschedule, please reach out once by phone or email and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

By booking a visit, you are acknowledging and accepting this cancellation policy.

Kids Policy

  • No strict age minimum, but mobile children (who can walk or move on their own) must have a paid admission.
  • Adult supervision is required at all times.
  • We require one responsible adult (18+) for every three children.
  • Teens (ages 14–17) may visit alone with a signed waiver from a parent or guardian.

Lounge Etiquette

To keep the experience safe and enjoyable for everyone (especially the cats!), we kindly ask all guests to follow these guidelines:

  • No carrying cats aroundYou're welcome to hold them, but please don't walk around with them in your arms.
  • Guests under 13For safety, we ask that younger children don't pick up the cats, but they're encouraged to gently pet and play.
  • Keep toys low & slowSudden or high movements can startle cats. Keep toys close to the ground and move them gently.
  • Respect cat napsPlease don't disturb sleeping cats. Gentle pets are fine, but let them rest.
  • Use your inside voiceCalm, quiet voices help create a peaceful environment.
  • No running or climbingKitty furniture is for cats only. Walk calmly and keep all feet on the ground.
  • Let the cats come to youNever chase or force interaction. Let them choose what they want to do.
  • No feeding the catsPlease don't share café food or drinks (even if the cat insists 😸). Ask staff if you'd like to give them a treat.
  • Follow staff instructionsOur team is here to help ensure a great experience. Guests not following the rules may be asked to leave.

Adoption Information

If you fall in love with one of our feline residents, our staff can guide you through the process, which begins with an application and ends with a contract with our partner rescues.

$100 adoption fee, which includes
  • Spay/neuter: every cat is fixed before adoption
  • Microchip for added safety
  • Testing for common feline illnesses
  • Age-appropriate vaccines

Adopters also receive a complimentary adoption bag with food, treats, and toys to help your new kitty settle into their forever home!
