The Café

Coffees, teas, frozen treats and snacks. Free to enter and always open to the public, so grab a latte on your way to work.

Spend 50 minutes playing, relaxing, or just enjoying the cats' company. $13 per person.

Adoptions

Fall in love? Our partner rescues can help you take a furry friend home.

5,000+cats adopted into loving homes

Making a difference, one cat at a time

Since opening in 2017 as Cincinnati's first cat café, we've helped over 5,000 cats find loving homes. Every visit, whether it's a latte to go or an hour in the lounge, directly supports our rescue partners.

Not looking to adopt? You're still welcome! Whether you already have pets at home, live somewhere that doesn't allow them, or just love being around cats, our lounge is the perfect place to unwind.

Cat lounge: $13 for 50 minutes. Kids 15 and under are $7 on Wednesdays.
